# Kling 5 Prompt Guide

A short, practical guide to writing prompts for Kling-style AI video generation, based on experiments with [Kling5 AI](https://kling5.ai/), a web-based Kling 5 video generator for text-to-video and image-to-video clips.

## The four parts of a good clip prompt
1. **Subject**: who or what is on screen ("a red sports car", "a chef in a small kitchen").
2. **Action**: one clear motion ("drifts around a corner", "flips a pancake").
3. **Camera**: shot size and movement ("low-angle tracking shot", "slow orbit").
4. **Look**: lighting, lens and style ("golden hour, shallow depth of field, cinematic").

## Examples
| Use case | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Product ad | "A matte black wireless earbud case opens on a marble surface, macro shot, slow dolly-in, studio lighting" |
| Social post | "A golden retriever runs through autumn leaves toward the camera, handheld tracking shot, warm afternoon light" |
| Storyboard test | "Wide shot of a rainy neon street at night, a cyclist passes left to right, camera static" |

## Image-to-video tips
- Use a sharp reference image with the subject clearly separated from the background.
- Describe only the motion you want; the image already sets the look.
- Keep camera moves simple for short clips.

## Iterate
Change one variable per run (camera, light or action) and keep the generation history so you can compare versions.
